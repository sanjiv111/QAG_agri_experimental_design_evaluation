Experimental Design by AI Scientist for Paper 02 
Paper Title: Design of agricultural question answering information extraction method based on improved BILSTM algorithm Agricultural Domain: crop information.

Experimental Objective: To investigate whether transformer-based semantic models improve agricultural fact extraction and inference accuracy.

Dataset Description Source/Size/Type: Agricultural text corpus and benchmark datasets annotated for precision/recall/F1 evaluation. Preprocessing: Agricultural text corpus processed with embedding models (Word2Vec, GloVe, BERT).

Model Description A BiLSTM classifier is used for the agricultural QA tasks, with its input embeddings derived from different pre-trained models: Word2Vec, GloVe, and BERT.

Experimental Procedure:

Train embedding models (Word2Vec, GloVe, BERT).

Build BiLSTM classifier for agricultural QA tasks.

Compute precision, recall, and F1 scores.

Compare performance across embeddings.

Validate extraction accuracy on benchmark datasets.

Precision, Recall, F1.

"BERT-BiLSTM achieved the highest F1 score, outperforming Word2Vec-BiLSTM and GloVe-BiLSTM."

Evaluation Setup Metrics: Precision, Recall, F1. Task: Single-turn QA involving crop-sowing, region classification, and fact retrieval. Baselines: Word2Vec-BiLSTM and GloVe-BiLSTM served as baselines for comparison with BERT-BiLSTM.

Strengths

Investigation of transformer-based semantic embeddings to improve BiLSTM performance.
