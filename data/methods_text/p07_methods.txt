Methods Extract – p07 (AgriBERT / Agricultural NLP)

(Note: p07 includes combined QA + NER + NLP pipeline.)

Dataset Construction:
An agricultural corpus covering pests, diseases, crops, and farming processes is created through:

Collection of expert texts

Cleaning and normalization

Entity and relation annotation

Integration into a knowledge graph for QA tasks

Model Architecture:
AgriBERT is adapted from BERT with domain-specific pretraining on agricultural corpora.

NER and Relation Extraction:
The system extracts agricultural entity types (disease, crop, soil, chemical) for downstream QA tasks. It applies:

Transformer encoding

Contextual embeddings

Multi-layer classification

Post-processing for consistency

QA Task Pipeline:
Questions are matched to relevant entities; retrieved passages are fed into AgriBERT-based QA model to generate answers.
