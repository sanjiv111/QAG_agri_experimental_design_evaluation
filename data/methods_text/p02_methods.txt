Methods Extract – p02

Information Extraction Framework:
The system processes semi-structured and unstructured agricultural QA data. Semi-structured extraction uses XML-based rules to map elements to entity categories and attributes. For unstructured text, entity extraction is performed using HMM-based sequence labeling with BIOES tagging.

BERT–BiLSTM–CRF (BE-BiLSTM):
Text embeddings are generated via BERT. These embeddings are fed into a BiLSTM to capture forward and backward contextual signals. Outputs are concatenated and passed through a CRF layer to predict entity labels. This model extracts entities such as crop types, pest names, disease traits, and agricultural product trade details.

Relationship Extraction:
The BiLSTM output is further used to identify relations between extracted entities (e.g., crop ↔ pest, price ↔ quantity).

Conversion to Semi-Structured JSON:
Entity and relation outputs are mapped into predefined JSON templates for integration into databases or knowledge bases.

Experimental Design / Data Collection:
20,000 agricultural QA items (soil, pests, crops, trade) were collected from Malaysian agricultural websites. Data were split into 70% training and 30% test sets. Data annotation used BIOES tagging and manual correction. Three categories: soil, crop, disease.

Model Settings:
Batch size = 32. Learning rate tuned via search. The BE-BiLSTM configuration trains entity extraction and relation extraction jointly.
